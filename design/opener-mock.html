Forge opener: “Forged”

A star falls from the top left: a real stone, its leading face melting in the air, a dark blue aura and a glowing train behind it, its light hitting the ground as it comes. It lands at the centre, heats, and is forged into the ingot, which cools through orange, ember and iron to the Forge cube. Two cuts: the full film on a first launch, leading into the naming screen, and a short one for every launch after. Click the film or press Space to play or pause.

How it would work in the app

Which cut plays

Mock 1 on the first launch (no name saved yet), ending on the naming screen. Mock 2 on every launch after, ending in the app. Any key or click skips either one.

Native, not a video

Built in GPUI like the onboarding: the star, trail, ground and cube drawn as paths and quads, words as text. Reduced motion: no opener.

The blue aura is new

The star’s blues (#2f63c4 aura, #a9c9ff core, #16306e train) are the one colour not in the theme yet; the stone’s melt uses the heat ramp. It would join the heat ramp as tokens, used only by the opener.

Silent by default

Optional: a low whoosh as the star falls and one anvil strike on the lock, both off by default.
