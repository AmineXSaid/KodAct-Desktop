Forge opener: “Forged”

The ingot heats in the dark, from iron through ember and orange to white, locks white hot, and cools through orange, ember and iron to the Forge cube. Two cuts: the full film on a first launch, leading into the naming screen, and a short one for every launch after. Click the film or press Space to play or pause.

How it would work in the app

Which cut plays

Mock 1 on the first launch (no name saved yet), ending on the naming screen. Mock 2 on every launch after, ending in the app. Any key or click skips either one.

Native, not a video

Built in GPUI like the onboarding: the cube, glow, ground and sparks drawn as paths and quads, words as text. Reduced motion: no opener.

Only theme colours

The heat is the ingot’s own ramp (white, glow, ember, iron) and the cooled cube its own faces; the dark is a warm near-black under the stage colour.

Silent by default

Optional: a low roar as the ingot heats and one anvil strike on the lock, both off by default.
