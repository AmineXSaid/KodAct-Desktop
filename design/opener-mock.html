Forge opener: “Forged”

A 7.6-second brand teaser that plays as the desktop app opens. Molten sparks spiral in, the ingot is forged white hot, cools through orange, ember and iron while Forge’s loop slams in three lines, settles into the wordmark, then opens onto the app. 16:9, 128 BPM grid, silent by default. Click the film or press Space to play or pause.

How it would work in the app

Native, not a video

Built in GPUI like the onboarding: the ingot, sparks and rings drawn as paths and quads, words as text. Sharp at any size, tiny, and it follows the theme (the iris opens onto light or dark).

Never in the way

Any key or click skips it. Plays on the first launch and after an update, not on every start (an option in Settings). Reduced motion: no opener.

Silent by default

An app that plays music each time it opens wears thin. Optional: one anvil strike on the lock, off by default.

Only what Forge does

The three lines are its real loop, and the closing line is the app’s own copy. Colours are the theme’s tokens: the heat ramp, the teal, paper and charcoal.
