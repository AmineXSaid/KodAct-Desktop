Forge logo: six directions

Each one is drawn for the app's real sizes (16px message heads, 24px working line, 32px taskbar), and works in one colour, in reverse, and as the turning “working” logo. All six use the new palette: ivory, charcoal and patina teal.

Today’s mark: a photographic voxel ore cube with glowing blue veins. It’s rich, but it’s the one illustrated, glossy thing in a flat, quiet UI. At 16px its detail turns to noise, and its blue now fights the teal. Every direction below keeps one idea from it (the cube, the blocks, or the glow) and drops the rest.

My pick: 01, Ingot

It's the smallest change that fixes the problem. It's still Forge's cube, so nobody has to relearn the brand, but now it's flat and drawn in the app's own palette, and it reads at 16px. It's the only one where the existing “working” animation (the cube turning a quarter at a time) carries over unchanged, now drawn in vector instead of 72 PNG frames.

Runner-up: 04, Voxel F, if you want the logo to say the name. Avoid using 06 alone: a wordmark needs one of the others as its small-size mark.
