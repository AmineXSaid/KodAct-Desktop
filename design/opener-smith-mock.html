Forge opener: “The strike”

The welcome art from Forge for VS Code comes to life. The smith slowly draws the hammer back over his shoulder, brings it down fast on the cube, the cube bursts into its voxels, and the pieces carry the transition: on a first launch they rebuild the Forge cube over the naming screen; for a returning user the whole thing is a quick strike and they rebuild into the app. Click the film or press Space to play or pause.

How it’s built. The night scene is the real welcome art (assets/welcome-art-source.png) with the smith and the cube cut out. The smith is redrawn as a jointed block figure in the same black-and-white dotted pixel style, so the arm can swing from the shoulder; the cube is built from 48 voxels so each can fly. Everything is drawn on a 3 px grid and every frame is a function of time, so it can be rebuilt natively in GPUI. Any key or click skips; reduced motion: no opener. Dark only in this mock.
