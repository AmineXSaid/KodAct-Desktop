# Identity critique — KodAct CLI and KodAct desktop, 2026-11-05

Assessment A only (native GPUI app and a terminal UI; the web detector does
not apply). Evidence: the CLI built from `AmineXSaid/KodAct` main and run in
xterm (dark and light, 24-bit colour); the desktop app at `faf34e2` (after the
rename) on Xvfb, light and dark. Sources: the CLI's `tui/palette.rs`,
`tui/brand.rs`, `tui/front.rs`, `tui/render.rs`; the desktop's `theme.rs`,
`ui/ingot.rs`, `ui/opener.rs`, `ui/activity.rs`, `fonts.rs`, PRODUCT.md.

## Verdict

The CLI has a real identity. The desktop app has Forge's identity with
KodAct's name on it. Moving between `kodact` and the desktop, nothing but the
word carries over: two products, two companies.

## What the CLI owns (carry it over)

1. **The name says the mechanism.** The wordmark sets "Kod" in paper and
   "Act" in lime: code, then act. The strongest asset either product has.
2. **The mark.** A lime tile, corners rounded, holding an ink cursor bar and
   a run triangle. Same idea as the wordmark, legible at 16 px, unlike
   anything in the category.
3. **Ink, paper and lime.** Lime `#c6ff3d` on ink, deepened to olive
   `#4a7200` on paper; teal and violet either side of it for an answer's
   structure; peach for inline code; green, red, amber only for state. No
   other coding agent owns lime (orange-terracotta, black, blue and purple
   are taken).
4. **Square pixels as material.** Wordmark and mark are built on a pixel
   grid, with a one-pixel olive drop shadow.
5. **Voice.** "Code, then act, on the model you choose." Plain and sure.
6. **State vocabulary.** ◆ live, ✓ done in lime, ✗ failed, the working
   verb shimmering from grey up to lime.

## Where the CLI's identity is weak

- **[P1] The light wordmark goes flat.** The sage shadow sits at about
  1.35:1 on paper, and olive "Act" loses the lime's energy; on light, lime
  survives only in the tile.
- **[P1] Lime is overloaded.** Brand, the default mode, commands, keys,
  selection and ✓ done are all lime, while SUCCESS is a second green
  (`#7ccf8f`) close in hue. Brand and "done" can't be told apart.
- **[P2] The brand record contradicts the product.** The CLI's PRODUCT.md
  still names "its pixel-art F mark" and "the orange accent"; the shipped
  TUI is lime with the cursor-and-run tile.
- **[P2] The 2x wordmark (11 rows) outranks the input** on every launch (the
  CLI's own critique agrees).
- **[P3] Half blocks depend on the terminal's font**: gaps show as lines in
  some fonts. A desktop app can draw those pixels exactly; it is where the
  pixel identity can look its best.

## The desktop app

- **[P0] It's Forge's world.** An isometric copper "ingot" for a mark, a
  hammer on an anvil for activity (`activity.rs`), an opener that heats the
  ingot to "830 °C" with sparks and a shockwave, an "ember copper" accent,
  a theme still called "KodAct Ember", cream paper. Not one CLI asset: no
  lime, no tile, no Kod/Act split, no pixels.
- **[P0] Borrowed identity.** PRODUCT.md names Claude Desktop as its
  quality reference; every word is set in Anthropic Sans (Anthropic's own
  face, loaded from a private folder, not open-licensed: against CLAUDE.md
  rule 1). Cream ground, terracotta accent and that face are Claude's look.
  The CLI's brief says the opposite: nothing borrowed from the reference
  CLI, and not its look.
- **[P1] The rename's wordmark is a stopgap.** "K◆DACT" in capitals with the
  isometric cube as the O is neither the CLI's wordmark (KodAct, mixed case,
  "Act" in lime) nor its mark.
- **[P1] A film on every launch.** 2.6 s each time, 8.3 s on the first: an
  orchestrated load sequence on a tool people open all day, themed on
  forging metal.
- **[P2] Primary actions disagree.** Allow is copper, every other primary is
  an ink pill, and DESIGN.md says primaries are accent with 4 px corners.
- **[P2] Inline code is red** while DESIGN.md says "never coloured".
- **[P2] "Rules and memory" describes Claude Code's files**, which KodAct
  doesn't load as instructions (it reads KODACT.md and AGENTS.md).

## Direction

One brand across both: the CLI's identity, translated for a desktop app,
not a terminal costume.

- **Palette:** ink, paper and lime as the default theme; teal and violet for
  an answer's structure, peach for code; status colours apart from the brand
  lime.
- **Mark and wordmark:** the CLI's own pixel glyphs and tile, drawn exactly;
  "Kod" in ink or paper, "Act" in lime.
- **Signature move:** the mark at work. The cursor bar is what is live (the
  working line's bar, the shimmer to lime); the run triangle is Send.
- **Type:** an open-licensed face of KodAct's own for the interface, a mono
  for code, paths and keys only.
- **Motion:** states only; no launch film.
