## Priority issues
1. [P1] Menus behave differently from one another: Esc fails on + and mode menus; the project chip navigates; the design menu re-anchors and its tabs jump; the tooltip draws over the open mode menu. Fix: one Popover primitive (Esc, click-outside, focus, anchored by its top edge); a real project menu; no tooltip while a control's menu is open.
2. [P1] Settings uses 3 controls for one kind of choice (segmented, check chips, radio cards) and 2 primary colours (black pill vs copper). Fix: segmented for 2–3 short options, radio list with descriptions, chips only for filters; one primary style.
3. [P1] Unfinished surfaces shipped: Network "Planned" page; Skills cards empty or "Drawing…"; Design search scoped to one tab ("pitch deck" finds nothing in Skills). Fix: hide Network; typographic covers; search all tabs with per-tab counts.
4. [P2] Typed syntax where a form belongs: /routine grammar, Bash(npm test) rules, env placeholder that looks like real data. Fix: routine form, rule builder with match preview, "e.g." placeholders.
5. [P2] No signature of its own: pick mock direction A/B/C; one signature through every surface; motion down to 3 durations.
