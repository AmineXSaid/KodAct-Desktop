# opener: Forge's first-launch film

- **Type:** brand teaser, played as the desktop app opens for the first time.
- **Format:** 1920x1080, 16:9, silent, 128 BPM grid, 6 bars plus a hold (12.9 s).
- **Message:** Describe it. Forge builds it. You approve. Ends on "Forge asks
  before it changes anything." and the naming screen.
- **Assets:** the app's fonts and tokens; the ingot geometry. No photos.

## Chapters

| Bars | Chapter | Showpiece |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Star | A raw star in a teal aura falls from the top left over a dot-field floor, lighting it. |
| 2 | Land | It lands on the anvil: a ring through the dots, a shake. "Describe it." It glows into a white-hot ingot. |
| 3 | Strike | A hammer (the smith off frame) winds up slowly and strikes fast: flash, sparks, ring, shake. |
| 4 | Shatter | The ingot bursts into 64 voxels that fly up and hang, cooling. "Forge builds it." |
| 5 | Approve | The voxels come back together into the Forge cube; its top cools to teal. "You approve." + check. |
| 6 + hold | Lockup → naming | The wordmark and the app's line; the cube rises into the naming screen's hero. |
